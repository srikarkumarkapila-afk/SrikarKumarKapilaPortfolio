William Mason High School Football Team • Student Drone Pilot

Experiential Reflection

Last year, I had the opportunity to become the first ever student drone pilot for the William Mason High School Football Team. I spent 15 hours per week taking care of the drone. I would record practices 3 days of the week. On Friday evenings, I would stream my footage to the Team to provide live insight on the game. Later, I would personally asked the coaches for feedback.

The experience was not only eye opening for me on how much effort the sports team puts in, but also taught me responsibility. One of the times I recall: on a fine day, I was flying the drone, when a battery error caused it to malfunction mid air. Dropping from a height of 100ft, the drone violently crashed. I took complete responsibility by explaining to the coaches what exactly happened. Seeing my dedication, the coaches received permission to buy a new one. After hours of research, I found the best drone that was optimal for the price and purchased it. I came right back on track and finished the season strong.

That experience paved my way to not only deal with intense pressure on my schedule, but also with situations that I could not control. I learned the value of integrity, responsibility, effort, and time. In daily life, unexpected or intense situations occur all the time. By practicing these values and skills, I grow more robust and handle these situations faster and more efficiently.

This experience showed me that, as a Global Citizen Scholar, I must be ready for all kinds of situations. Sometimes, the solution I come up with could have a very bad side effect–but, I know now that if I am dedicated, I can fix it. A Global Citizen Scholar must be reliable, adaptable, and responsible.
